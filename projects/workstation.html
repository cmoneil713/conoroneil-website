Personal Project

Powered Workstation

The Concept

The goal was to make an engineering station as ergonomic as possible - optimized to the task at hand.

The structure uses telescoping steel members and four powered linear actuators to move the work surface. The desktop PC, UPS, and additional peripheral components are mounted to the frame so the system remains self-contained and mobile.

Design Priorities

Actuation

Powered actuation integrated into a heavy-duty steel frame on wheels.

Integrated PC and UPS

CAD workstation hardware and backup power mounted onto the structure.

Optimized for Engineering

Designed for CAD, assembly, soldering, sketching, and all sorts of tinkering.
